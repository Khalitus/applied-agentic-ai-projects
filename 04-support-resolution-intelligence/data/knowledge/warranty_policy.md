# Warranty Policy

## Standard coverage
Products are covered for manufacturing defects during the product-specific warranty period shown in the product catalog. Coverage starts on the original purchase date.

## Eligible cases
Manufacturing defects, component failure under normal use, and verified hardware faults are eligible when the product remains inside the warranty period.

## Exclusions
Accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repair, liquid damage, and cosmetic wear are not covered by standard warranty.

## Resolution
Support should verify purchase date and product warranty duration. Eligible cases may receive repair or replacement. Premium customers with repeated unresolved warranty cases should be considered for escalation.
