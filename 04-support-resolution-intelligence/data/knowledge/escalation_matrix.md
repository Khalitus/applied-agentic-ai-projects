# Support Escalation Matrix

## High-priority escalation signals
Urgent priority, security incidents, payment fraud indicators, repeated unresolved contacts, premium customers with service failure, and high-value orders may justify escalation.

## Repeated contacts
Three or more recent support contacts for the same unresolved problem should increase escalation consideration.

## Human judgment
Model scores are decision support only. Support staff remain responsible for the final escalation decision and should review the ticket context and applicable policy.
