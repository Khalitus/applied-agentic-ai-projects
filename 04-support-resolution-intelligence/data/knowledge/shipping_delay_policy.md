# Shipping Delay Policy

## Delay threshold
A shipment is considered materially delayed when tracking has not progressed for 5 business days beyond the expected movement window.

## Resolution
Support should first review tracking history. If the delay threshold is met, offer an updated ETA, replacement shipment, or refund according to order status.

## Escalation
Premium customers, high-value orders, and repeated failed deliveries may be escalated when the normal resolution path does not restore service.
