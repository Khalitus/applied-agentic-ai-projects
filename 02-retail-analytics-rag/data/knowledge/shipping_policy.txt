NORTHSTAR RETAIL — SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY
Document ID: POL-SHP-2025
Effective date: 1 January 2025

1. Standard delivery targets
South region: 2 to 3 business days.
Central region: 2 to 4 business days.
North region: 3 to 4 business days.
West region: 4 to 6 business days.

2. Corporate orders
Corporate orders containing five or more computers require stock confirmation before a
delivery commitment is made.

3. Delayed shipments
A shipment is considered delayed when delivery exceeds the upper end of the regional
target by more than one business day. Delays must be escalated to Operations.

4. Same-day retail pickup
Orders marked Retail Store are treated as store pickup and normally have zero delivery days.

5. Customer communication
For confirmed delays, customers should receive an updated delivery estimate and a reason
for the delay.
