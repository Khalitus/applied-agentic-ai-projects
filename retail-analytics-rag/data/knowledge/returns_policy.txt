NORTHSTAR RETAIL — RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY
Document ID: POL-RET-2025
Effective date: 1 January 2025

1. Standard return window
Customers may return unopened Accessories, Audio, Displays, and Storage products within
14 calendar days of delivery when proof of purchase is available.

2. Computers
Computers may be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery only when the product is
unopened and the factory seal is intact.

3. Defective items
A product reported as defective within 7 calendar days must first be verified by technical
support. If the defect is confirmed, the customer may receive a replacement or refund.

4. Opened products
Opened non-defective products are generally not eligible for a cash refund. A store credit
may be approved by a customer service supervisor when the item is in resalable condition.

5. Final-sale items
Clearance items explicitly marked FINAL SALE are not returnable unless they are defective.

6. Refund timing
Approved card and digital-wallet refunds are normally initiated within 3 business days.
Bank-transfer refunds may take up to 5 business days after approval.
