NORTHSTAR RETAIL — DISCOUNT AUTHORIZATION POLICY
Document ID: POL-DSC-2025
Effective date: 1 January 2025

1. Standard discounts
Sales representatives may approve discounts up to 5 percent without additional approval.

2. Manager approval
Discounts above 5 percent and up to 10 percent require Sales Manager approval.

3. Commercial approval
Discounts above 10 percent and up to 15 percent require Commercial Head approval.

4. Maximum normal discount
The normal maximum discount is 15 percent. Any exception above 15 percent requires a
written exception approved by the Finance Director.

5. Enterprise pricing
Enterprise orders may use negotiated pricing, but the approval level is still determined by
the effective discount percentage.

6. Audit requirement
The approver and reason for any discount above 5 percent must be recorded.
