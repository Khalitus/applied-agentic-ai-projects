NORTHSTAR RETAIL — CUSTOMER SERVICE PLAYBOOK
Document ID: OPS-CS-2025
Effective date: 1 January 2025

1. Low ratings
Orders with a customer rating of 2 or below should be reviewed by Customer Experience
within two business days.

2. Returned orders
Customer service must record a return reason and link the case to the original order ID.

3. Escalation
Repeated delivery complaints involving the same region, supplier, or product family should
be summarized and escalated to Operations.

4. Evidence
When answering customer questions about policy, staff should quote or reference the
relevant policy document rather than relying on memory.

5. Data privacy
Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and payment credentials must never be added
to analytics exports used for training or demonstration purposes.
