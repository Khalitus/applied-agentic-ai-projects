NORTHSTAR RETAIL — COMPANY POLICIES
Effective date: 1 January 2025

==================================================
1. RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY
==================================================

Standard return window:
Customers may return unopened Accessories, Audio, Displays, and Storage products within 14 calendar days of delivery when proof of purchase is available.

Computers:
Computers may be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery only when the product is unopened and the factory seal is intact.

Defective items:
A product reported as defective within 7 calendar days must first be verified by technical support. If the defect is confirmed, the customer may receive a replacement or refund.

Opened products:
Opened non-defective products are generally not eligible for a cash refund. A store credit may be approved by a customer service supervisor when the item is in resalable condition.

Final-sale items:
Clearance items explicitly marked FINAL SALE are not returnable unless defective.

Refund timing:
Approved card and digital-wallet refunds are normally initiated within 3 business days.
Bank-transfer refunds may take up to 5 business days after approval.

==================================================
2. SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY
==================================================

Standard delivery targets:
South region: 2 to 3 business days.
Central region: 2 to 4 business days.
North region: 3 to 4 business days.
West region: 4 to 6 business days.

Corporate orders:
Corporate orders containing five or more computers require stock confirmation before a delivery commitment is made.

Delayed shipments:
A shipment is considered delayed when delivery exceeds the upper end of the regional target by more than one business day. Delays must be escalated to Operations.

Same-day retail pickup:
Orders marked Retail Store are treated as store pickup and normally have zero delivery days.

Customer communication:
For confirmed delays, customers should receive an updated delivery estimate and a reason for the delay.

==================================================
3. DISCOUNT AUTHORIZATION POLICY
==================================================

Standard discounts:
Sales representatives may approve discounts up to 5 percent without additional approval.

Manager approval:
Discounts above 5 percent and up to 10 percent require Sales Manager approval.

Commercial approval:
Discounts above 10 percent and up to 15 percent require Commercial Head approval.

Maximum normal discount:
The normal maximum discount is 15 percent.

Exceptions:
Any exception above 15 percent requires a written exception approved by the Finance Director.

Enterprise pricing:
Enterprise orders may use negotiated pricing, but approval level is still determined by the effective discount percentage.

Audit requirement:
The approver and reason for any discount above 5 percent must be recorded.

==================================================
4. CUSTOMER SERVICE PLAYBOOK
==================================================

Low ratings:
Orders with a customer rating of 2 or below should be reviewed by Customer Experience within two business days.

Returned orders:
Customer service must record a return reason and link the case to the original order ID.

Escalation:
Repeated delivery complaints involving the same region, supplier, or product family should be summarized and escalated to Operations.

Evidence:
When answering customer questions about policy, staff should quote or reference the relevant policy document rather than relying on memory.

Data privacy:
Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, and payment credentials must never be added to analytics exports used for training or demonstration purposes.
